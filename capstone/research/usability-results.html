Usability Testing and Results

A significant observation is that even though I included an instruction to click on the picture frame to view a thorough result, many people did not know. They would either ignore the end of the instructions or forget about it. The overlay would pop up when they try to drag and drop the stickers and would get confused so it did not help and made them confused.

Another significant observations is when the users drag and drop the stickers, the stickers would overlap one another. The stickers would also drop outside of the picture frame if they dropped it too early. The users would they would try to move the stickers again but would not be able to since it is one of the features. I will need to fix these problems.

A third significant observation is when the users would also try to drag both options of sticker for a question. They would want to choose both answers because they can not make a decision. I will need to code to only allow one option to be drag and dropped.

Can users figure out how to navigate/interact with your project?

Yes, the users understood that they have to drag and drop the stickers to the picture frame for each question. They also knew that they had to scroll to answer more questions. One interaction that was not indicated as clear as I would like is to click the picture frame for an overlay of a thorough result.

Does the project impact the user the way you want it to? (Does it have the right message or communicate what you want it to?)

Yes, the project impacts the user the way I want it to. The users are able to answer questions and see their potential desired future.

What modifications need to be made to interactions, visuals or other elements of the project to make it stronger?

I will need to make improvements on the stickers by fixing the overlapping of stickers when it is dragged and dropped onto the picture frame, change the colors to indicate it is a different option, and be able to hover the sticker and see an animation to indicate it can be chosen. I will also include icons or some images to bring visuals into my project.

After recieving feedback, I plan to fix the problems of the stickers and change its colors and add a hover feature like animation to indicate it can be chosen. I will also make the unchosen sticker options opacity drop to indicate it was not chosen. This will also tell he users that it cannot be drag and dropped also. I also plan to add icons next to the stickers so users can have a visual of the options. I will add an instruction to click on the picture frame for a more thorough result at the bottom of the page.
