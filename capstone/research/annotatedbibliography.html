Creating Your Future In 5 Easy Steps

The main focus of this website is creating your future in 5 steps: declare, dream, design, delete, and do. When you create a life of reality, you end up less happy than if you had lived your dream life. The author's conclusion is to live your dream life, it is never too late and following the 5 steps can help you achieve your dream. I also think that it is possible to live your dream life if you constantly work towards it. For my project, I may create my questions based on these 5 steps to help the users create their dream life.

Structuring Your Future

When we think about our future we should understand our values, do the backward-looking approach, create a list of what you want to look back at with satisfaction, allow yourself to refine your list over a period of time and adapt and evolve to it as years go by, then use your list regularly as a decision-making tool, and review it and repeat. The author’s conclusion is to spend your life knowing that when you look back, you are satisfied with your past and your decisions. I also think it is crucial to do the backward-looking approach because people tend to stress over the future and if they will be successful rather than making sure they will be happy and satisfied. For my project, I may alert the users to do the backward-looking approach as they complete the questions,making sure that they choose the answers they will be satisfied with.

Structuring Your Future: A Proven Method for Personal and Professional Planning. https://spaf.cerias.purdue.edu/planning.html#top.

10 Questions To Ask Your Future Self

This article gives you 10 questions to ask your future self. Since thinking about the future can be overwhelming and stressful, these 10 questions can help you start thinking about your future rather than keep you from stressing out. After answering the questions, reverse engineer them and think about what you can do right now. The author wants people to think about these questions stress free and work through them slowly and not make the perfect plan. I also agree because the future is unpredictable so working through the questions can help you start to picture what you would like and slowly achieve them. For my project, I will use these questions as examples of what I can ask the users about their future.
