Project Overview

My capstone project, Design Your Future! is an interactive project that allows users to go through a series of questions to choose which option they would want their future to be like. Users will be able to choose their desired future by dragging and dropping the answers (icons) to each question in the picture frame. The users can view the the picture frame through icons and also through a paragraph when you click on the picture frame.

The Target audience for Design Your Future! are young adults who are either graduating or thinking about their future. The future is unpredictable and most people do not know what they will do in the future so these series of questions will allow them to see what their desired/ potential future can look like.

The goal of Design Your Future! is to allow users to picture their future through the results they chose. They may look at the picutre frame and either rethink their future plans/ goals or love it and strive for the best future. It can give users a sense of direction to take towards their future.

Project Research

For my research, I began by searching up articles about creating and structuring your future as well as questions to ask about your future. When you design your future, you would want to live your dream life and be able to look back at your life with satisfaction. When I first thought about what questions to ask, I thought about asking only simple questions like "Where would you like to live in the future?", but after researching, I decided to ask questions that will allow you to look back with satisfaction.

Next, I worked on comparative analysis research. I found interactive websites that were similar to interactive ideas I had for my project. The first website is DVEIN which has a zoom in and out, draw, and camera feature. The second website is Playground Paris which allows users to drag and drop images to place them anywhere. It allowed me to explore interactive elements I did not think about, expanding ideas for my project. I thought about Thinking about my project, I decided to include drag and drop so that users can interact with the icon and the picture frame as well as be satisfied with their answers when they look at their finished picture frame.

Visual Design

The visual design shows a bit of a futuristic theme. I decided to use silkscreen for the title font, space mono for the header, and DM sans for the body font. I also chose a black background with brighter neon text. They give my project a futuristic theme. I also split the screen in half with questions on one side and the picture frame on the other side so that users can easily drag and drop and see their future as they decide what options they want to choose.

After user feedback, I decided to make the options icons since the overlay results is a paragraph about your future already. I also made the chosen sticker pop out more and made the picture frame glow once you submit the series of questions to indicate that you can click on the picture frame.

User Interface

I wanted my project to be interactive so users will drag and drop the icons onto the picture frame in order to answer the question. I wanted the stickers to be icons instead of words so that they can view the picture frame like a picture and be able to look at a descriptive paragraph when they click on the picture frame. This allows there to lessed the cognitive load in the picture frame.

I also featured a hover on the icons using tippy.js so users can know what each icon represents. I thought that including the desctiption as a hover will also lessen the cognitive load but at the same time ensure that people will know what the options are to thw questions.

I made the picture frame sticky so it stays on the screen and makes it easier for users as they scroll and drop their icons onto the picture frame.

Revisions

After getting feedback from user testing, each person mentioned adding images or something visual to my project. Alexis suggested using icons which I thought was a good idea so I incorporated icons for my final version. Chao suggested a hover state or another feature to the picture frame to tell users to click on it when they are done answering the questions. I thought about how I can tell users to click on the picture frame without using words and thoguht that a flashing light on the picture frame can help.

I learned that allowing other people to test your proejcts allows you to see flaws. You can watch how the users think and choose to do to figure out if your project is understandable and user-friendly. To further develop the project, I would like to allow users to be able to submit their own picture frames and view other user's results too.

Summary

While working on this project, I was able to explore different libraries to use for my project. I was amazed by how many libraries there are and how helpful they can be for projects. I am proud of being able to incorporate interactive elements to the project to make it engaging for users. It was challenging to provide data of what users chose and display it on the overlay but it feels rewarding after figuring it out.
