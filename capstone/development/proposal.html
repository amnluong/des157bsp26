Capstone Proposal

For my capstone project, my idea is to design your own future. It will show how one answer can affect/ change your life. The users will choose answers to questions about their life and each answer will lead to another possible future. This project will rely on speculative design since the users are choosing their future of possible what ifs. It may also include utopian or dystopian narratives since the answers you are choosing may give you a future you may like or dislike.

Some features my project my include may be a quiz and a result of what your future may look like. The results may have a scrolling feature that uses AOS library with interactions that walks through your possible future. You can take the quiz again or change an answer to see how your future changes I am not sure what other libraries I will use, but I may use libraries with a lot of interactive features. I am not sure what questions are good to ask to determine the future and/ or what important parts in life I should include. I also need to figure out how I would represent the future result.

My target audience would probably be young adults who are trying to figure out what they want to do in their life. It is a big transition from school to full adulthood so I feel like young adults are the most curious about the future. It can also tell users that even though we need to make decisions, we will still be fine in the end and do what we like.
